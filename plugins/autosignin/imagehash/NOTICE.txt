AutoSignIn Tjupt image helper

Pillow mode conversion/resampling algorithms are translated from Pillow12.2.0;
PILLOW-LICENSE.txt retains its license. Exact source pins and adaptations are
in ORIGIN.json. The jpegport subpackage retains the Go JPEG decoder and
libjpeg-compatible algorithm notices/licenses in that directory.

The pure Go BMP/WebP codecs and CCITT primitives use golang.org/x/image
v0.24.0. XIMAGE-LICENSE and XIMAGE-PATENTS are retained. JPEG2000 Part1 privately relocates decoder-only source from
github.com/mububoki/jpeg2000 v1.0.0 with OpenJPEG2.5.4 binary32 arithmetic;
jpeg2000port/LICENSE and OPENJPEG-COPYRIGHT.txt retain MIT/BSD notices.
TIFF decoder-only files are privately relocated from github.com/hhrutter/tiff
v1.0.7, with Pillow sample/polarity/JPEG adaptations. tiffport/LICENSE and
ORIGIN.json retain the upstream BSD license and exact source inventory.

webp.go translates the limited YUV conversion/fancy upsampling leaf of
libwebp1.6.0 src/dsp/yuv.h and upsampling.c. Copyright2010-2011 Google Inc.
LIBWEBP-COPYING, LIBWEBP-PATENTS and LIBWEBP-AUTHORS retain upstream notices.
No native codec library, CGo or Python runtime is required by the product.

AVIF bitstreams use github.com/gen2brain/gav1d v0.2.6, a pure Go dav1d
port. GAV1D-COPYING/PATENTS retain its BSD2 notices and AV1 patent text.
The pinned AVIF container decoder in avifport exposes the native planes
before color conversion. avifport/ORIGIN.json records the local adaptation.
The RGB helper translates fixed libyuv YuvPixel and ScaleRowUp2 leaves.
AVIF coded-plane scaling follows libyuv ScalePlane and its ratio filters.
LIBYUV-LICENSE/PATENTS/AUTHORS retain upstream notices. The scalar conversion
and chromaticity calculation follow libavif 1.4.1 reformat.c and colr.c;
LIBAVIF-LICENSE retains its BSD-2-Clause terms. Other default-image
format readers retain Pillow source attribution, and four selected MIT
Picsetgo leaves retain legacycodec/PICSETGO-LICENSE.txt and PROVENANCE.json.
This is finite source-derived input evidence, not a certification of the full
generic Pillow Image.open/save/seek/animation API or every codec variant.
